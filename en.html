SmartMark · Privacy Policy

Effective: October 1, 2026 ｜ Version v1.3.9

1. Our commitment

SmartMark is a local-first bookmark organizer. Your bookmark data is processed in your browser by default. We do not collect, upload, or sell any of your personal data. No ads, no tracking code.

2. Data we process

  • Bookmark data (titles, URLs, folder structure): read and organized locally for classification, deduplication, backup, and dead-link detection. Backups are stored in your browser's local storage (chrome.storage.local).
  • AI configuration (endpoint, model name, API key): stored only in your browser, used solely to call the AI service you configured. We cannot see your key.
  • Custom rules and categories: stored locally only.

3. Where data goes when AI is enabled

When you enable AI classification with your own configured endpoint, the extension sends bookmark titles and URLs to that endpoint (e.g., local llama-server, Agnes, DeepSeek, OpenAI, or your custom endpoint) to receive classification suggestions. Data sent is governed by your chosen provider's privacy policy.

If you use only the built-in rules engine (offline mode), no data leaves your browser.

4. Permissions

  • bookmarks: read and organize your bookmarks (core feature).
  • storage: store settings, backups, and rules locally.
  • activeTab: read the current tab title for auto-categorization of new bookmarks (optional).
  • host_permissions: connect only to the AI endpoint you configured.

5. What we never do

  • No collection of device identifiers, browsing history, or personal identity.
  • No third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.
  • Your data is never used for AI model training.
  • No sharing with third parties (except the AI endpoint you configured yourself).

6. Data security

All data lives in your browser's local storage. Uninstalling the extension clears local data (please use Settings → Export to back up first).

7. Children's privacy

This extension is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their information.

8. Changes

Policy updates will be noted in the extension changelog; material changes will be announced in-extension.

9. Contact

For privacy questions, contact us via the Support link on the Chrome Web Store listing.
